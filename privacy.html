Slime Brawl - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Applies to Slime Brawl on Android (package me.convertini.slimebrawl), Windows, Linux and the browser version.

Short version

Slime Brawl has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Settings, key bindings and match replays are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Online play

Local matches and LAN matches do not contact any server run by the developer.

When you create or join an online (crossplay) room, the game connects directly to the other players (peer-to-peer, WebRTC, encrypted). To set up that connection:

  • the game connects to a signaling server (signal.convertini.me, run by the developer behind Cloudflare) that relays the connection messages between the players of the same room: the room code, a random session id and the technical network details needed to connect (IP addresses and ports, WebRTC offers/answers). Like any internet server it sees your IP address. The messages are only passed on while the room is open: there is no database and nothing is kept, apart from the server software's own short-lived technical log (connection events), used only to keep the service running;
  • the game asks public STUN servers (Cloudflare stun.cloudflare.com, Google stun.l.google.com) for your public address, as every WebRTC application does;
  • when a direct connection is not possible, the traffic may be relayed by a TURN server of Cloudflare. The relay only forwards the encrypted game packets and does not read them.

The other players in your room receive your IP address (needed for a direct connection), the in-game name shown in the lobby (on Android always the default "player") and your game inputs during the match. There is no chat.

The game never sends your name, e-mail, contacts, location, device identifiers or any other personal data, and contains no third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics.

Cloudflare and Google process network traffic under their own privacy policies: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy, policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/DrDamnation/slimebrawl-site/issues.

In breve (italiano)

Slime Brawl non ha account, pubblicità, statistiche né tracciamento; lo sviluppatore non raccoglie, non conserva e non cede dati personali. Impostazioni e replay restano sul dispositivo. Nel gioco online la connessione tra i giocatori è diretta (WebRTC, cifrata); per collegarli, il server di signaling signal.convertini.me (via Cloudflare) inoltra i messaggi di connessione (codice stanza, indirizzi IP e porte) senza conservarli (nessun database; solo il breve log tecnico del server, usato per farlo funzionare), i server STUN pubblici di Cloudflare e Google rilevano l'indirizzo pubblico e, se serve, un relay TURN di Cloudflare inoltra i pacchetti cifrati. Gli altri giocatori della stanza vedono il tuo indirizzo IP, il nome in lobby ("player" su Android) e i tuoi input di gioco. Non è un gioco destinato ai minori di 13 anni.
